# HITOFUDE — STYLE GUIDE FOR CHAPTER WRITERS

Read series.md and cast.md first. This file is the enforcement layer.

---

## 0. BUBBLE DISCIPLINE

- **Four to eight words per bubble, hard ceiling.** If it won't fit, it's two bubbles or it's cut.
- **8–14 bubbles per page, default 10–12.** Density comes from the NUMBER of bubbles, never the length of one.
- **Three bubbles per panel maximum**, and three is rare — two is the house rhythm.
- Panels: **7 per page is the default, 8 often, 6 sparingly** — six is a floor, not a target. Big panels are for food, hands, and the ninth floor.
- A page of two people in a small room saying eleven short things beats a splash page every single time. (The chapter still carries its one mandated splash — put it where the emotion peaks, and give it 3–6 bubbles.)
- **Nobody explains their feelings.** They explain the rice bran. If a line states an emotion, rewrite it as an instruction about an object.

## 1. HONORIFICS

Keep Japanese honorifics untranslated. Rank overrides age in address, always.

**The register ladder (this is the romance's progress bar — spend it slowly):**

| Stage | Sayo → Kaoru | Kaoru → Sayo | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `Fujinami-shi` | `KIKUCHI` (window only) | ch1 |
| 1 | `Fujinami-shi` | `Kikuchi` | ch2 (he read it off paper) |
| 2 | `Fujinami-san` | `Kikuchi` | ch3, after the shop |
| 3 | **`Fujinami-kun`** | `Kikuchi` | **ch7, mid-rush, accidental** |
| 4 | `Fujinami` | `Kikuchi` | ch9–10 |
| 5 | — | **`Sayo`** | **ch12, at the sink, about a plate** |

`-shi` (氏) is the school's cold bureaucratic suffix for Ranks 7–9. It is technically respectful and functionally a wall. **Every downgrade of a suffix is a plot event.** Never advance a rung inside a dramatic speech; advance it mid-chore, mid-sentence, by accident.

Other rules:
- Ibuki says `Fujinami-shi` every morning for twelve chapters and never once gets it back.
- Kanta says `Sayo` and always has. He is the only one. Do not draw attention to it until ch12.
- Kotoha says `onii-chan`, and calls Sayo `Sayo-nee` from ch7 onward without asking anyone.
- Genzō uses no honorifics for anyone. He calls Kaoru "the barrel boy" until ch9, then "Kaoru," once, and never explains.
- Nobody has a nickname. This series does not do nicknames.

## 2. HOW THE SYSTEM SPEAKS

The Tally's windows are lettered in a **hard monospace/UI face**, always inside a thin single-weight box, never a bubble, never a tail.

```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│ FUJINAMI, KAORU          │
│ RANK 9  ·  SEAT 1 / 41   │
└──────────────────────────┘
```

**Rules, absolute:**
1. **ALL CAPS.** Family name first, comma, given name.
2. **Four lines maximum.** Usually two.
3. Punctuation allowed: `·` `/` `:` `+` `-` `—`. Nothing else. No exclamation marks, ever.
4. **It never says "you."** It has no second person.
5. **It never explains, apologises, congratulates, or warns.** It reports.
6. **It is never wrong and it never understands.** That is the entire characterisation. Its cruelty is always accidental and always accurate.
7. It does not appear in a panel unless someone is *looking*. Windows are a POV effect. In a panel drawn from Sayo's POV of an empty arcade, there are no windows in the world, and the page should feel like an exhale.

**Verdict lines — the killers. Use sparingly, never two in a chapter:**
```
RESULT: NULL
NO MAGICAL ACTIVITY DETECTED
1 STROKE / NO EFFECT
ADJUSTMENT: 0
PENDING
NO DATA
ACCESS: FLOOR 1
```

**Never write:** a window that is sarcastic, a window with a personality, a window that anyone hacks, disables, argues with, or beats. The System is weather. You do not defeat weather.

## 3. SFX

Romaji, then the English in parentheses on the same tag. Keep them short and keep them domestic — this series has more kitchen SFX than combat SFX and that ratio is the brand.

**System (fixed vocabulary — never vary these three):**
- `PIN (chime)` — a window opening. One syllable, cold, everywhere, all day.
- `SU (fade)` — a window closing.
- `PIN... PIN... PIN...` — the Turn, at 06:00, spreading across a city block.

**Domestic:**
`GORI GORI (grinding)` whetstone · `JAA (running water)` · `KOTO (set down)` · `MUNI (soft crunch)` pickled radish · `JIRI (sizzle)` · `PASA (cloth)` · `KACHA (dishes)` · `SHAAA (rain on the annex roof)` · `KARARA (shutter going up)` · `POKO (rice cooker)` · `FUU (breath on hot food)`

**Body / feeling:**
`DOKI (badump)` · `DOKUN (heavy badump)` — use `DOKI` at most twice in the whole season and never on a confession page · `ZAWA (murmur)` a corridor turning to look · `SHIIN (silence)` · `PIKU (twitch)`

**Signing:**
`SU—— (draw)` a stroke going down · `SHAKI (seal)` a shape closing · `PAN (discharge)` · `PACHI (spark)` one stroke, all Sayo ever gets · `POTA POTA (drip)` a failed cast falling apart · `SHAKI` is also the blade draw, deliberately — the same sound for sealing a sigil and unwrapping a knife. Nobody in-world notices. The reader will.

**Rule:** a page may go SFX-free as a deliberate hush (ch9's stairwell, ch12's last panel) — but SFX-free never means dialogue-free. THERE ARE NO SILENT PAGES: every page carries speech bubbles, a hushed page carries three, never zero.

## 4. RECURRING GAGS — seed these early, pay them late

1. **The vinegar question.** Someone asks what that smell is. Kaoru's answers degrade across the season: *"Reagent."* → *"A technique."* → *"Brine."* → *"Tuesday's."* → (ch11, to a proctor, without looking up) *"Radish."* Once per chapter minimum, three words maximum.
2. **The chore chart.** Laminated, on the shop wall, marker on a string. From ch7 a second handwriting appears on it. Nobody mentions it, ever, including in ch12. It is in the background of eleven chapters and it is the whole romance.
3. **"NO DATA. Best rank there is."** Genzō deploys it in every conversation regardless of relevance, including at a hospital, including at a funeral flower stand, including to a bus driver. Pays off in ch9 when he says it and it isn't a joke.
4. **Kanta's bread.** A different failed bakery experiment every chapter, in a paper bag with a handwritten tag naming it: *Regret* · *Wednesday, Structurally* · *Ambition* · *The Less Said* · *Almost* · *Finally* (ch12). The name is always a comment on the chapter and Kanta has no idea.
5. **Machi's pages.** She answers in handwriting held up from stairwell C. The page is always funnier than the scene it's in, and once a season it is not funny at all.
6. **Sayo's third person.** "Sayo's fine!" in public, `ore` in private. Track every slip. The public slip in ch11 is a season-level event, so nobody else may slip before it.
7. **The Shuffle.** Monday desk-dragging as a cold open. Use it in ch1, 4, 8, 12 — same panel composition each time, so the reader clocks what has changed by the fourth.
8. **The hot cabinet.** The konbini's half-broken hot-drinks cabinet dispenses the wrong thing to everyone all season. In ch11 it gives Kaoru the corn soup, and he hands it over without comment.

## 5. MUTTERED ASIDES

The private face leaks in the margins. Strict rules:

- **No bubble.** Small hand-lettered text floating beside the mouth, or a ragged/dotted outline at most.
- **Six words maximum.**
- **Always true.** An aside is never a joke the character is performing — it is the thing under the performance. If it would work as a spoken line, it isn't an aside.
- **Never acknowledged.** No other character may hear, repeat, or react to a muttered aside. Ever. One exception in the entire season: ch6, when Kaoru answers one, and that is what breaks the chapter open.
- **Ration:** three per chapter, per character, absolute maximum. Two is better.
- **The leads mutter in opposite directions.** Kaoru's asides are *warm* (his public face is cold). Sayo's asides are *cold* (her public face is warm). If you ever write it the other way round you have swapped the characters and the chapter is broken.

Examples:
- Kaoru, in class, looking at nothing: *...she didn't eat again.*
- Sayo, thanking someone brightly: *say that again. say it again.*
- Kaoru, at the sink: *too much salt. sorry.*
- Sayo, apologising: *forty-one. still forty-one.*

## 6. BANNED — do not write these

- **Sayo never gets magic.** Not in a burst of emotion, not at a climax, not "it turns out she was suppressing," not ever. Season-level, series-level, franchise-level ban.
- No hidden bloodline, no chosen one, no prophecy, no "the Tally has been waiting for you."
- **No villain explains the System's origin.** Nobody knows. Nobody ever finds out. Any writer who cracks this open has broken the premise.
- No tsundere denial construction (*"I-it's not like—"*). Sayo is not a tsundere; she is two competent people.
- No accidental gropes, no falling-into-a-kiss, no tripping-into-the-bath, no towel gags.
- No beach chapter, no hot spring chapter, no "we all got sick and had to be nursed."
- No slapstick violence as affection — nobody hits anyone for blushing.
- No amnesia, no coma, no terminal illness, no dead parent revealed for leverage. Kaoru's grandmother is dead before page one and is never used as a lever.
- No love triangle. Sui is not a romantic rival. Ibuki is not a romantic rival. Neither of them will ever be one.
- No "the power of friendship overrides the System." The System is not overridden in season 1. It is simply, occasionally, out-lived.
- No fireworks confession at the school festival. There is no school festival. Kudan has an Ascent instead, and the Ascent has a canteen queue afterwards.
- No character says "I've never had anyone be kind to me before." Nobody in this series is that unguarded, and it is a cheap line.
- No monologue longer than four bubbles. If a character starts one, put a kettle in the room.

## 7. CHAPTER SHAPE — the checklist

Every chapter must have, or it does not ship:

- [ ] **A domestic beat.** Food, laundry, a bandage, a bus, dishes. Not decorative — the scene's actual business happens over the chore.
- [ ] **At least one window that is technically correct and emotionally wrong.**
- [ ] **One gag from section 4**, run and paid inside the chapter.
- [ ] **A social-cost blocking beat** — where somebody sits, who speaks first, which floor, who eats alone. Blocking, not narration.
- [ ] **Both leads out of costume at least once**, even for a panel.
- [ ] **A funny page and a quiet page, in that order.** Comedy first, then the knife. Never the reverse.
- [ ] **Fight beat: default to none.** Two chapters in twelve get a real one. If you're reaching for a fight, you haven't found the chore yet.
