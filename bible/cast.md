# HITOFUDE — CAST

Season 1. Two leads, seven supporting.

**Uniform baseline (all students):** dark high-collar jacket, straight trousers or skirt, white under-shirt. On the **left cuff**: horizontal white **rank bars**, one per rank, stacked up the forearm. Rank 9 has nine bars nearly to the elbow. Rank 1 has one. This means rank is legible in line art with the HUD switched off — use it constantly. How a character wears their sleeve is characterisation.

---

# LEAD 1 — FUJINAMI KAORU

**藤浪 薫** · 17 · second year, Kudan Academy · **RANK 9** · Seat 1

Youngest Rank 9 in the academy's records until eight months ago. Universally called *the Ninth*. Lives above Fujinami Tsukemono in Sakaechō Arcade with his nine-year-old sister. Their grandmother died last spring; he has run the shop since.

### PUBLIC FACE
Glacial. Speaks in three to five words. Answers questions and does not ask them. Sits alone at Seat 1 with his back very straight because he was taught to. Has never been observed eating. Faintly, permanently smells of vinegar and something fermented, which the school has decided is an alchemical reagent, a family technique, or a curse. Rolls his sleeves to the elbow — nine bars fully exposed — which the entire school reads as the single most arrogant gesture available to a human being.

He is not cold. Nobody is allowed to speak to him first. He stopped starting conversations six hundred days ago because they only ever went one way, and the silence set.

### PRIVATE FACE
An anxious, fussy, deeply uncool domestic animal. Cat-print apron. A laminated chore chart on the shop wall with a dry-erase marker on a string. Labels every crock in small careful handwriting with the date and a comment (`TUESDAY. GOOD.` / `TUESDAY. HMM.`). Talks to the *nukadoko* — the fermented rice-bran bed — the way other people talk to a dog, including scolding it and apologising to it in the same minute. Hums, badly, one song, always the same one. Worries about salt content the way other people worry about death.

He rolls his sleeves because he washes his hands about forty times a day.

### VOICE
Public: declarative, no particles softening anything, sentences that end early. Private: fast, fussy, imperative, full of small domestic nouns, and he apologises to objects. The tell that he's dropped his public face is that his sentences get **longer**, not warmer.

**SAMPLE LINES — public**
- "Move. You're in the door."
- "It's fine. Don't."
- "That's floor six."
- "I wasn't looking at you."
- "...Understood."
- "No."
- "Ask a proctor. Not me."
- "Your hand's bleeding. Fix it."

**SAMPLE LINES — private**
- "You're too salty today. Explain yourself."
- "Did you wash your hands. HANDS."
- "The lid was loose. The LID, Kotoha."
- "Don't stir clockwise, you animal."
- "I labelled it. See? Tuesday. TUESDAY."
- "Eat something before you fall over."
- "Sorry, sorry, sorry— hot, hot—"
- "It's a radish. It has feelings about salt."

### ARTIST LOCK
**FUJINAMI KAORU** — 178cm, tallest of the students except Machi; lean, flat-shouldered, long forearms, stands too straight like someone corrected him for years. Hair: **solid black, no tone**, blunt straight fringe cut slightly too short across the brow (he cuts it himself, over the shop sink, badly), and a single stubborn cowlick at the crown that never lies down — always draw the cowlick, it is his whole personality escaping. Eyes: narrow, heavy-lidded, **very small dark pupils with almost no highlight**, lower lash line drawn as one flat stroke — reads as bored, is actually mild social panic. Faint permanent shadow under both eyes. Uniform: worn correctly to the millimetre, collar hooked, jacket buttoned to the throat, but **both sleeves rolled to the elbow** exposing all nine white rank bars on the left cuff. Props: (1) a **shop-issue folding knife** in his breast pocket, plain wooden handle, used exclusively on vegetables; (2) a **hand towel** tucked at his hip, always, even in uniform — nobody at school has ever worked out why. **Private-face tell:** the **cat-print apron** (fat white cat, screentone-grey stripes, one strap always twisted) over the school shirt with the collar hook undone and the cowlick doubled — plus, in private, his hands are drawn *in motion*, wiping, stirring, wringing; in public they are drawn still at his sides. Public = still hands. Private = busy hands. That is the switch.

# LEAD 2 — KIKUCHI SAYO

**菊池 沙世** · 17 · second year, Kudan Academy · **RANK 1** · Seat 41 / 41

Lives above Kikuchi Hamono, the knife shop, with her grandfather. Up at 04:10. Sharpening delivery route through the arcade and the north bank restaurants by bicycle before school. Stroke Count 1. Has never been above the first floor of a nine-floor school.

### PUBLIC FACE
Sunny, hopeless, harmless. Apologises before anyone can be disappointed in her. Laughs at herself first so nobody else has to do it and feel bad about it. Refers to herself in the third person — "Sayo's fine at the back!" — which is a costume, and an effective one: it makes her a small friendly object rather than a person you would have to accommodate. Sits in Seat 41 by the recycling bin. Eats in the annex, at the table under the leak, alone, in all weather, cheerfully.

The performance is airtight. It has to be. Rank 1 that isn't likeable doesn't get left alone; it gets *managed*.

### PRIVATE FACE
Cold, profane, and unforgiving. Uses **ore**. Keeps a notebook in her jacket ranking her classmates — not by rank, by how annoying they are, with commentary, updated weekly, and it is vicious. Has enormous physical competence and a contempt for people who don't: she can halve a falling receipt, feel a burr on an edge through a cloth, and put a chef's knife back to true in ninety seconds. Forearms. Trains on the river steps at 04:40 with a wrapped stick because a live blade in public would be a Ledger event.

Her wall is simple and permanent: the Tally has no column for what she can do. The System does not measure edges.

She has an ugly small motive at the start and it is not romance — she wants, once, before she graduates, to stand on the ninth floor.

### VOICE
Public: third person, diminutives, trailing off, "ah—" at the front of half her lines, laughs mid-sentence. Private: **ore**, flat declaratives, imperatives, swearing that the letterer renders as ordinary words because the delivery is what's obscene. Her voice does not get louder in private. It gets *level*.

**SAMPLE LINES — public**
- "Ah— sorry, sorry, sorry!"
- "Sayo's fine at the back! Really!"
- "No no, take mine, take it—"
- "Sayo's not really, um. You know."
- "Eheh. That's Sayo for you!"
- "Please don't put me in your group."
- "It's not cold! It's character-building!"

**SAMPLE LINES — private**
- "Move, or lose the hand."
- "That edge is a crime. Who taught you."
- "I'm not sorry. Not even slightly."
- "Nine floors. I've seen one."
- "Don't be nice at me. Don't."
- "Hold it like a chopstick. Chopstick."
- "One stroke. Best in the prefecture. Useless."
- "Ore wa— ...ah. Ahaha. Sorry."

### ARTIST LOCK
**KIKUCHI SAYO** — 158cm, twenty centimetres under Kaoru; compact and low-centred, with **visibly developed forearms and wrists** that the uniform hides badly — draw the forearms honestly, they are the plot. Hair: **mid-grey screentone (60%)**, chin-length blunt bob, side-parted, two cheap plastic clips over the right ear holding the fringe out of her eyes; the tone must read instantly against Kaoru's solid black in any two-shot. Eyes: **large, round, high-set with two full highlights** and a soft lower lid — maximum harmlessness. In private, draw the same eyes with **the highlights reduced to one small hard dot and the lower lid flattened**; nothing else changes and it changes everything. Uniform: jacket a size too big (grandfather bought it with room to grow, four years ago), **left sleeve always pulled down over the knuckles** to cover her single rank bar, hem frayed from being gripped. Props: (1) a **canvas roll of sharpened knives** strapped to the bicycle rack, wrapped in blue shop cloth; (2) **bandaged fingers** — never the same finger twice, always at least one, plus whetstone grit permanently in the creases of her palms. **Private-face tell:** the **hair goes up** — a twisted knot held with a pencil or a chopstick and a faded blue shop bandana across the brow, sleeves shoved to the elbow with the single white bar bare and unhidden, feet planted wide. Public = sleeve down, hair down, hands hidden. Private = sleeve up, hair up, hands out. Mirror of Kaoru's switch, deliberately.

# SUPPORTING CAST

## TSUJIMURA IBUKI
**辻村 いぶき** · 17 · second year · **RANK 8** · Seat 2

Class representative, socially fluent, genuinely well-meaning, which is what makes her the season's most effective source of pain. She *champions* Sayo publicly and it is a catastrophe every single time.

**Public:** immaculate, warm, fast, remembers everyone's name and one fact about them.
**Private:** cannot be alone. Sleeps with the television on. Eats convenience-store egg sandwiches standing over the sink at 11pm because sitting down to eat alone is the specific thing she can't do. Terrified of being ordinary; Rank 8 is close enough to the top to know exactly how far it isn't.

- "Ah— Kikuchi-san! Perfect timing!"
- "I'll handle it. Obviously I will."
- "That's so like me, sorry, sorry."
- "Fujinami-shi. Good morning. ...Good morning?"
- "You could try, though. Couldn't you?"
- "...The sink's fine. Sinks are fine."
- "Don't tell anyone I said that."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 165cm, upright, gestures with both hands. Hair: **near-white / heavy highlight**, long, half-up, a ribbon that matches nothing; big silhouette, reads as light against both leads. Eyes: large, bright, **three highlights**, thick upper lash — expensive-looking. Uniform: flawless, pressed, skirt exactly regulation, eight rank bars kept spotless with a lint roller she carries. Props: (1) a **clipboard** with the class list; (2) a **lint roller** in her bag. **Private-face tell:** drawn at night in an oversized grey sweatshirt with the sleeves past her hands, hair completely flat, eating standing up, screen-glow lighting from below.

## MEGURO KANTA
**目黒 寛太** · 17 · second year · **RANK 3** · Seat 22

Sayo's only friend, loud, cheerfully mediocre, entirely at peace with Rank 3. Part-time at a bakery. The character who says the true thing by accident and then doesn't notice he said it.

**Public:** volume.
**Private:** writes extremely earnest poetry about a coworker and will die before showing it, which means he mentions it constantly.

- "OI! Bread!"
- "This one's called Regret. It's got yuzu."
- "Rank three's plenty, man. Plenty."
- "You two are weird together. It's nice."
- "I wrote a poem. Don't read it. Don't."
- "You never eat with anyone."
- "...huh. That came out sad."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 172cm, broad, slouched, hands in pockets or holding a paper bag. Hair: **white/blond-read**, cropped short with an undercut growing out unevenly. Eyes: small, wide-set, permanently mid-laugh, thick brows. Uniform: jacket open over a T-shirt, three rank bars, tie nonexistent. Props: (1) a **paper bakery bag**, always, with a handwritten tag; (2) flour on one sleeve. **Private-face tell:** a **cheap notebook** he shoves into his waistband whenever anyone approaches, and drawn with his ears solid black.

## AMENOMIYA SUI
**雨宮 翠** · 16 · first year · **RANK 9** · Seat 1 (first-year hall)

Took "youngest Rank 9 on record" from Kaoru eight months ago. Charming, luminous, adored. The season's antagonist-shaped pressure, and not a rival for anyone's affection — she wants something worse than that. She wants Kaoru to *care* that she beat him, and he genuinely does not, and it is destroying her.

**Public:** delightful prodigy.
**Private:** practises until she vomits, then rinses and continues. Keeps a notebook containing every recorded cast of Kaoru's since he was fifteen, indexed. Cannot conceive of a self that is not a number.

- "Fujinami-shi. Nine to nine."
- "I've watched all of them. All of them."
- "You're wasting it. It's obscene."
- "Threw up twice. It's fine."
- "What is *she* for?"
- "Look at me. Look at the number."
- "...Why don't you want it."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 152cm, smallest in the cast, slight, always drawn from a low angle so she looms anyway. Hair: **very pale grey tone (20%)**, short, sharp asymmetric cut, one side tucked behind the ear. Eyes: enormous, **pupils drawn as tiny black dots in a lot of white** — bright and slightly wrong; the whole face is a beautiful child's and the eyes are a machine's. Uniform: worn perfectly, first-year piping, **nine bars on a very small forearm** — the visual gag and the visual threat. Props: (1) an **indexed black notebook**; (2) a water bottle she never puts down. **Private-face tell:** drawn kneeling on practice-hall floor, hair sweat-flat, one hand braced, the notebook open beside her — and her strokes, in that state, come out **forked and dripping**.

## FUCHIDA MACHI
**淵田 まち** · 18 · third year · **UNLISTED (Rank 0)**

Fell off the roll two years ago. The System renders **no window at all** for her — not a blank one, none. Eyes slide off her. Eats in stairwell C. Enormous, silent, gentle.

**Public:** does not exist, functionally.
**Private:** extremely funny, and communicates by holding up a page of her sketchbook. Fills it with drawings of people's faces with the windows deliberately left out — everyone in Nagihama, rendered as a person instead of a number. She speaks aloud twice in the season. Both times it lands like a building falling over.

Her "lines" are pages held up. Letter them as handwriting, not bubbles.

- *[page: "HE'S STARING"]*
- *[page: "HE'S STILL STARING"]*
- *[page: "you're loud for a rank 1"]*
- *[page: "sit. i have crackers"]*
- *[page: a very good drawing of Sayo, no window]*
- *[page: "don't. it's fine down here"]*
- (aloud, ch9) "You're allowed to be relieved."
- (aloud, ch12) "Draw me when I'm gone, then."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 180cm, tallest character in the series, heavy-shouldered, rounded, moves slowly. Hair: **solid black**, very long, unstyled, hanging forward in curtains that hide one eye and most of the face; **negative space is the design** — she should read as a dark shape first. Eyes: when visible, wide, calm, soft-lidded, low highlights. Uniform: third-year, worn loose and untucked, **left cuff bare — no bars at all**, which is the most alarming detail in the book. Props: (1) a fat **sketchbook**, rubber-banded, corners furred; (2) a box of crackers. **Private-face tell:** she pushes the hair back behind one ear with a pencil to draw, and the whole face is suddenly there and suddenly young — use it three times all season, never more.

## KIKUCHI GENZŌ
**菊池 玄三** · 74 · **NO DATA** (Unmeasured)

Sayo's grandfather. Kikuchi Hamono, est. 1948. Hands have begun to shake, which he has told nobody, including himself. Painted the NO WINDOWS AT THIS COUNTER sign in 1997 and has been asked to remove it eleven times.

**Public:** gruff shopkeeper who overcharges people he dislikes.
**Private:** cries at the radio.

- "NO DATA. Best rank there is."
- "Steel doesn't read."
- "Chopstick. Not a hammer."
- "Your grandmother hated me."
- "Sharp is a verb."
- "Sit down. You're vibrating."
- "That boy smells like a barrel."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 160cm and stooped, thick through the hands and wrists, everything else thin. Hair: **white, screentone-free, drawn as line only**, cropped, plus three days of stubble. Eyes: nearly closed by default, drawn as two curved lines; when they open the whites are drawn and it's a shock. Wears a shop coat over a vest, sleeves cuffed with rubber bands, **no rank bars anywhere because he was never issued any**. Props: (1) a **whetstone worn into a saddle-shaped hollow**; (2) reading glasses pushed up on his head and hunted for daily. **Private-face tell:** he tucks his shaking right hand into the opposite armpit whenever anyone comes in — draw it in the background of a dozen panels before anyone mentions it.

## ICHINOSE MANABU
**一ノ瀬 学** · 34 · **RANK 7** · homeroom teacher, class 2-C; Tower proctor

Reads the seating list aloud every Monday morning. Has done it four hundred times. Administers the machine, believes in it about forty percent, and is exhausted by the remaining sixty.

**Public:** procedural cheer. Never editorialises.
**Private:** a desk drawer of confiscated objects he has never returned or thrown away, and a folded paper list — kept since his second year of teaching — of students the System got wrong. Smokes on floor 5 because 9 is not his. Doesn't sleep.

- "Seats, everyone. It's Monday."
- "I don't write the list."
- "...I do read it, though."
- "Kikuchi. Forty-one. As you were."
- "Don't make me put that in a window."
- "Best single stroke I've seen in twenty years."
- "That's not a compliment. That's the problem."

**ARTIST LOCK** — 176cm, thin, permanently slightly stooped forward at the neck from reading lists. Hair: **grey-black tone (40%)**, receding at the temples, flattened on one side. Eyes: tired, half-lidded, deep lower shadow, small round glasses with **the lenses toned solid grey** in every public panel. Wears a cheap suit with the jacket off and sleeves rolled, faculty band on the arm, seven bars. Props: (1) a **printed class list on a clipboard**, curling at the corner; (2) an unlit cigarette behind the ear. **Private-face tell:** **glasses off**, pinching the bridge of his nose, the eyes drawn clear with visible whites for the first time — used exactly when he is about to say something honest.

**藤浪 琴葉** · 9 · **PENDING**

Kaoru's sister. The only person who has ever seen him cry, a fact she deploys strategically and often. Excited beyond words about her tenth birthday, when her window activates. Everyone else in the room goes quiet when she brings it up, and she has not noticed yet.

**Public/private:** the same child in both, which is the point of her and the reason her chapters hurt.

- "Onii-chan cried once. Once."
- "I'm PENDING! Look! PENDING!"
- "Is she your girlfriend or your employee?"
- "You do the voice. Do the radish voice."
- "Grandma's crock is the loud one."
- "What if I'm a one?"
- "...is that bad?"

**ARTIST LOCK** — 130cm, small and round-limbed, always in motion or asleep, never in between. Hair: **solid black like Kaoru's**, long, in two low uneven bunches she ties herself, with **the identical crown cowlick** — the family resemblance is one line of ink. Eyes: huge, round, **four highlights**, maximum shine; the brightest eyes in the book by a distance. Wears a primary-school smock over shorts, indoor shoes worn outdoors. Props: (1) a **stepstool** she drags everywhere in the shop; (2) a much smaller matching cat apron. **Private-face tell:** she has none. She is the only person in Nagihama with one face, and every adult in a panel with her is drawn slightly turned away.

# RELATIONSHIP MAP — season start

**Who knows whose secret at season start: nobody.** That is the whole starting position and it must be defended in the writers' room. Not one person in Nagihama knows either lead's private face on page one of chapter one.

**Partial exceptions, stated precisely:**
- **Kotoha** lives with Kaoru's private face and does not know it *is* a secret. She is a live grenade for eleven chapters.
- **Genzō** has watched Sayo sharpen since she was six. He knows she is good. He does not know about the notebook, the temper, or the ninth floor.
- **Ichinose** has a paper list with her name on it. He knows the System is wrong about her. He does not know what she actually is.
- **Machi** sees everything, from stairwell C, and tells nobody, because nobody asks her anything.

**Orbits**
- **Sayo → Kaoru:** proximity, then irritation, then obligation, then choice. Not admiration. Never admiration.
- **Kaoru → Sayo:** she is the first person in fourteen months who spoke to him first, and she did it by accident, at 5 a.m., while calling him an idiot.
- **Ibuki → Sayo:** wants to save her; is standing on her.
- **Ibuki → Kaoru:** the only person who greets up, every morning, and gets nothing, and does it again.
- **Kanta → Sayo:** the only friendship that predates the story. Load-bearing.
- **Sui → Kaoru:** obsession, indexed, alphabetised.
- **Sui → Sayo:** contempt that becomes a question.
- **Machi → Sayo:** the floor below her, which Sayo is ashamed to find comforting.
- **Genzō → Kaoru:** grudging, competitive, and by chapter 7 openly delighted.
- **Kotoha → Sayo:** instant, total, and mortifying for both siblings.
