# HITOFUDE — SEASON 1 ARC

Twelve chapters, 26–32 pages each (25 is the absolute floor — add scenes, never compress). Fights: two real ones (ch8, ch11) and one small ugly scuffle (ch5). Everything else is talking, food, and stairs.

**Secret schedule (locked):**
- Ch1 — first accidental glimpse, no knowledge
- Ch3 — Sayo *knows* Kaoru's private face
- Ch6 — Kaoru knows Sayo's; mutual
- Ch7–10 — deepening, domestic
- Ch11 — both private faces surface in public and they choose not to retreat
- Ch12 — warm landing, Tower stakes raised for season 2

---

## CH 1 — "SEAT FORTY-ONE"

**Premise.** Monday. The Shuffle. Sayo drags her desk to the back corner by the recycling bin for the fourteenth month running while apologising to three separate people for the noise. At the front, Seat 1, the Ninth sits alone in a bubble of enforced silence nobody will cross. Casting practicum: Sayo produces one stroke. Ichinose stops mid-sentence, because it is the most beautiful single stroke he has seen in twenty years — and one stroke closes nothing, so the window prints `1 STROKE / NO EFFECT` and the class moves on in under four seconds. That night she does not sleep well and gets up at 04:10 anyway.

**Domestic beat.** The 04:10 route. Rice cooker set the night before. Bicycle chain slipping in third. The blue cloth roll of finished knives lashed to the rack. Canned corn soup from the konbini's half-broken hot cabinet, held against the sternum for the warmth before it's drunk.

**Ground gained.** None. That's the design.

**Magic beat.** The practicum. Use it to teach the whole system in three panels and to teach the reader what one stroke is worth.

**Final page.** 05:12, Sakaechō Arcade, shutters down, nobody alive. The Ninth of Kudan Academy is on the pavement in a **cat-print apron** with both arms in a bucket of rice bran, saying, out loud, "You're too salty today. Explain yourself." Sayo's brakes.

## CH 2 — "WHAT'S THAT SMELL"

**Premise.** She cannot unsee it. She also cannot say it — who would she tell, and with what standing? Meanwhile the class's long-running theory about the Ninth's vinegar smell escalates (reagent? family technique? something he keeps?). She drops her delivery slip in the corridor; he picks it up, reads the name off the *paper* rather than off her window, and says four words to her. The corridor stops. Ibuki's clipboard hits the floor. It is the largest social event of the term and nothing happened.

**Domestic beat.** Lunch in the annex, February, under the known leak. Hand cream worked into knife-callused knuckles under the table where nobody can see. Kanta arrives with a bread called *Regret*.

**Ground gained.** He knows her name — and, crucially, learned it from paper. Small, and she notices, and hates that she noticed.

**Magic beat.** None. Instead: the SPEAKING QUEUE renders on the homeroom wall and Sayo talks anyway and no eyes move. Draw the eyes not moving.

---

## CH 3 — "NUKADOKO"

**Premise.** She goes to the shop. She tells herself it's to return a slip. It isn't; the slip is hers. He is behind the counter in the apron with a chore chart on the wall behind him and there is no version of this either of them can walk backwards out of. So he hands her a wooden paddle and makes her stir the rice bran, because his hands are wet, and because a man who has not spoken to anyone in fourteen months has forgotten how to ask people to leave. She is furious about how good the pickles are.

**Domestic beat.** The whole chapter. The bran bed, the smell (SFX and reaction shots, never description), the paddle, the daikon, the labels, the laminated chart, tea. She eats standing up and says "...oh," which is the chapter's emotional peak.

**Ground gained.** **SECRET 1 FALLS.** Sayo knows Kaoru's private face. They negotiate terms in the flattest possible register: *"Nobody hears about this." / "Obviously not."* Neither of them says why they're both relieved.

**Magic beat.** Tiny and devastating. He reheats the tea with one lazy stroke without looking at it, the way you'd flick a light switch. She watches him spend a thing she has never had on a cup of tea. Say nothing about it. Draw her face.

---

## CH 4 — "GREET DOWN, NEVER UP"

**Premise.** Group Assessment draft. Ibuki, out of genuine kindness, publicly champions Sayo for her group in front of everyone — the average drops, everyone is *lovely* about it, and Sayo has to spend forty minutes being grateful. Then in the corridor, in front of the second-year hall, the Ninth says "Morning" to a Rank 1. Unprompted. Downward, which is permitted, but from *nine* to *one*, which is not done. The building loses its mind. Sayo has to survive an entire afternoon of people suddenly wanting to sit near her, for the wrong reason.

**Domestic beat.** Kanta's bread (*Wednesday, Structurally*). Sayo repacks her bento on the annex table into two halves without deciding why, then leaves half in a shop doorway on the way home, then goes back for it, then leaves it.

**Ground gained.** Sayo reframes him: his coldness is not a personality, it is a rule that was applied to him. She starts being angry on his behalf, which is a much more dangerous feeling than sympathy.

**Magic beat.** None.

---

## CH 5 — "FOUR IN THE MORNING"

**Premise.** He starts appearing on her route with a crate. Neither acknowledges it as a decision. Twelve minutes of walking, most of it silent, some of it about salt. Then a drunk from the north bank shoulders past Genzō outside the shop and puts a hand on the knife roll, and the thing is over in a second and a half: Sayo doesn't sign, doesn't shout, just moves — and afterwards there's a severed strap on the ground cut clean through in one pass and a man sitting down very suddenly. Kaoru reads it as luck. She lets him.

**Domestic beat.** Two people carrying one crate badly. Hot canned soup on the river steps at 05:40. He asks whether she's eaten today and it is genuinely the first time in the series anyone has asked her anything about herself.

**Ground gained.** He asks: *"What do you want."* Not "what's your goal" — what do you *want*. She says the ninth floor. Once. Out loud. To the person who owns it.

**Magic beat.** The scuffle — but written explicitly as the *absence* of magic. Two panels, no sound effects except cloth and one blade note. The System renders nothing at all, because nothing measurable occurred.

---

## CH 6 — "ORE"

**Premise.** A restaurant returns a knife she sharpened, complaining. Genzō takes the blame in front of the customer. Sayo watches her grandfather apologise for her work, discovers the burr is not hers, and something comes off its hinges — in the shop, at the counter, with Kaoru standing there holding a crock of pickles he brought over as an excuse. She goes level and quiet and profane and says **ore**, and then hears herself, and the mask comes back up about four seconds too late.

**Domestic beat.** The whetstone. Water, angle, the sound (GORI GORI). She sharpens his beat-up shop knife at the counter while refusing to look at him. Two pairs of ruined hands on one small counter — his soft and chemical-burned from brine, hers grit-lined and bandaged. Draw the hands. That's the love scene.

**Ground gained.** **SECRET 2 FALLS. Mutual.** He does not make a thing of it. He says something appallingly ordinary — *"Your other voice is better. Use it here."* — and then panics about the pickles.

**Magic beat.** None.

---

## CH 7 — "TWO APRONS"

**Premise.** Setsubun rush: the shop is buried, the shipment is late, and Kotoha has a school thing. Sayo works the counter for four hours in a borrowed apron. Kotoha arrives, sizes Sayo up in one second flat, and asks whether she's a girlfriend or an employee. Genzō comes looking for his granddaughter and ends up in the back room arguing about salt ratios with a seventeen-year-old for two hours, which is the happiest either of them has been all year.

**Domestic beat.** Effectively the whole chapter: change tin, wrapping paper, the good scissors, four people eating standing up in a back room at 9pm, dishes done by two people who have never done dishes together and immediately have a system.

**Ground gained.** The honorific drops. Mid-rush, mid-sentence, without ceremony: *"Fujinami-kun, where's the—"* and he answers before he registers it, and then registers it. The chapter's biggest moment is a two-letter suffix. No fight beat could touch it.

**Magic beat.** None. Deliberately the season's most magic-free chapter, immediately before the ascent.

---

## CH 8 — "ASCENT"

**Premise.** Winter Ascent, floors 6 through 9. Kaoru is required to climb; Sui declares an open challenge and gets it. Sayo's ID admits her to floor 1. She waits in the annex with everyone else who can't go up, watching the result windows tick down the stairwell one floor at a time, and the rule that has been a joke for seven chapters stops being a joke.

**Domestic beat.** She lets herself into the shop with the key he left under the crock, feeds Kotoha, and keeps a dinner warm for four hours by moving a pot on and off a low flame. He comes down at 21:40 with a split lip and eats all of it without speaking and then says thank you to the pot.

**Ground gained.** He comes to *her* floor afterwards instead of the roof, and that is the confession, and neither of them calls it one.

**Magic beat.** **THE SEASON'S BIG FIGHT.** Nine strokes against nine strokes in a practice hall on 9. Sui's work is baroque, layered, immaculate, and she is signing at the edge of her hand — forked terminals, dripping. Kaoru's is clean, boring, four strokes where hers is nine, and he wins by closing a smaller shape sooner. Afterwards the window prints `RESULT: FUJINAMI` and Sui reads it and asks him, quietly, why he doesn't want it. He doesn't have an answer. He has a shop.

---

## CH 9 — "THE UNMEASURED"

**Premise.** Genzō's hand shakes badly enough to cost him a finger's worth of skin, and a hospital visit exposes the ward that honours rank at triage. Meanwhile Sayo, hiding from her own homeroom, ends up in stairwell C, where Fuchida Machi has been eating lunch alone for two years, unrendered. Machi holds up a page. Then another. Then she says something out loud.

**Domestic beat.** Hospital vending machine, the wrong drink, hot towels, a fingernail-sized dressing done badly then redone. Later: Sayo maintaining Genzō's whetstones at midnight, flattening them on wet paper, because he can't and won't say so.

**Ground gained.** Sayo admits to Kaoru that she is relieved someone is below her, and that she has been relieved about it for two years. He does not absolve her. He says "yeah," and passes her a plate, and the not-absolving is the kindest thing anyone does all season. Machi's line — *"You're allowed to be relieved"* — should be the only quiet page break in the chapter.

**Magic beat.** The smallest possible use of Rank 9: one stroke, a warm patch, held under Genzō's wrist for eleven minutes on the bus so the old man's hand steadies enough to finish an edge. Nine bars on the cuff and he's using one stroke to be a hot-water bottle. Never comment on it in dialogue.

---

## CH 10 — "PENDING"

**Premise.** Kotoha turns ten. Her window activates at 06:00 with a chime, in the kitchen, with everyone standing there pretending to be normal. Kaoru has been quietly terrified for eleven chapters and has told no one, including his sister, including himself.

**Domestic beat.** A structurally unsound birthday cake made by two people who can both cook and neither of whom can bake. Sayo sleeping on the shop floor because the last bus went. Laundry on the balcony over the arcade at 6am. The good scissors used on wrapping paper and Kaoru's face about it.

**Ground gained.** Kaoru says the fear out loud — not about the rank, about the fact that whatever number lands, it will be the first sentence anyone ever says about her. Sayo says the true thing, which is short and not comforting and helps anyway. This is the chapter where they become a unit rather than two people hiding in the same room.

**Magic beat.** Kotoha draws her first stroke, wobbling, wet-ended, absurdly proud of it. Kaoru corrects her grip without thinking. Sayo watches a nine-year-old do something she never will and is, for about two seconds, unbearable — and shows it, and Kotoha doesn't notice, and Kaoru does.

**The number itself:** show it in the last panel or don't show it at all. Writers' room decision. Recommendation: `RANK 4`, unremarkable, and everybody exhales, and it's *still* awful, because now she has a floor.

---

## CH 11 — "SPEAK FIRST"

**Premise.** Sui, having failed to make Kaoru care, goes for the reason he doesn't: corners Sayo on the first-floor stairs with an audience, and makes it a formal challenge in front of a proctor so that Sayo cannot decline without it going in a window. And Sayo — Rank 1, at the bottom of the visible order, in front of the whole stairwell — greets **up** first. Deliberately. Out loud. Which is the actual transgression of the chapter, not the fight.

**Domestic beat.** After: Sayo bandaging her own hand one-handed in the annex, badly, with her teeth involved. Kaoru arriving, saying nothing, unwrapping the whole mess and doing it again properly, complaining the entire time about the tension. Two vending-machine drinks, neither of them the one that was wanted.

**Ground gained.** Both private faces are now visible in public — her real voice in a corridor, his hands on her hand in front of forty people — and neither of them retreats. Ibuki sees it and understands something about herself and says nothing.

**Magic beat.** **Sayo vs Sui.** Nine strokes against a wrapped stick. Sui's sigils are gorgeous and Sayo simply is not where they close, three times, and ends it by stopping the stick a centimetre off Sui's collarbone. Then the window renders:

```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│ NO MAGICAL ACTIVITY      │
│ DETECTED                 │
│ RESULT: NULL             │
│ ADJUSTMENT: 0            │
└──────────────────────────┘
```

She won and it did not happen. Sui stares at the window, then at Sayo, and the first crack in her whole worldview is that *she* knows what happened and the System doesn't. Hold on Ichinose taking his glasses off.

---

## CH 12 — "THE TURN"

**Premise.** Last Monday, 06:00. Citywide recalculation. The whole arcade is awake for it, pretending not to be. Sayo goes out to the river steps to look at it where her grandfather can't see her face: `RANK 1 · ADJUSTMENT: 0`. Then, ninety seconds later, every window in Nagihama renders the same notice.

**Domestic beat.** Dinner at the pickle shop with the shutter half down: rice, three kinds of pickle, Genzō and Kotoha arguing about the crocks, Kanta arriving uninvited with a bread called *Finally*. Machi at the end of the table with her sketchbook. Dishes. On the wall, the laminated chore chart now has a second handwriting on it, and has for weeks, and nobody has mentioned it once.

**Ground gained.** Warm. Landed. He uses her name — just *Sayo*, at the sink, about a plate — and she says "yeah," and that's all, and it's enough for a season.

**Magic beat.** None, except the notice:

```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│ KUDAN ACADEMY  ·  NOTICE │
│ CONSOLIDATION OF ROLL    │
│ EFFECTIVE NEXT ACADEMIC  │
│ YEAR                     │
│ RANK 1 AND UNLISTED      │
│ TO BE REMOVED            │
└──────────────────────────┘
```

Eleven months. Two names at that table.

**Last page.** Kaoru reads the sponsorship clause off his own window, out loud, flatly, while drying a plate: a Rank 9 may sponsor one student for an Open Ascent — and the sponsor's own rank is staked against the result. Sayo says *"No."* He says *"I didn't ask."* Machi holds up a page. It says: **"he's going to."**

---

## SEASON 2 TEASER

He is going to stake nine bars on a girl who cannot close a shape, and the only person who can talk him out of it is the one person the System will not let speak first.

Sui starts showing up at the shop at five in the morning, and will not say why, and stirs the rice bran wrong on purpose.

Somewhere above floor nine there is a tenth door, and it is not for a rank — it is for a name, and the Tally has never printed one.
