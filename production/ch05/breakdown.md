# CHAPTER 5 — FOUR IN THE MORNING

LOGLINE: For five mornings running the Ninth of Kudan Academy is standing under his own awning at 04:22 with a crate he does not need to move until six, and neither of them ever says one word about why he is there; then on Friday a drunk off the north bank puts a hand on the Kikuchi knife roll and it is over in a second and a half with no shout, no stroke and nothing whatsoever rendered in the air, and afterwards, over a can of tea that a crate of turnips has warmed to exactly sixty degrees, he asks her the first question anyone in Nagihama has ever asked her about herself, and then a second one, and she answers it out loud to the person who owns the answer.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Ch4 ended with Sayo quietly angry ON HIS BEHALF and saying so to nobody. The seven students who came down to the annex never came back; the annex is empty again here and nothing in the script comments on it.
- Honorific register stage 2 and it does NOT advance: Sayo says "Fujinami-san", the class says "Fujinami-shi". Kaoru has never said her given name and does not this chapter. Ch7 owns the next rung.
- Vinegar rungs spent: REAGENT / A TECHNIQUE / BRINE / TUESDAY'S. "Radish" stays locked for ch11. Kanta's breads so far: Bottom Shelf, Regret, Left In, Well Meant.
- Secret 2 is intact: Kaoru does not know Sayo grinds the Kikuchi blades, does not know the temper, and does not know about the ninth floor until page 24. Sayo has not slipped to "ore" in front of anyone and must not until ch6.
