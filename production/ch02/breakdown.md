# CHAPTER 2 — WHAT'S THAT SMELL

LOGLINE: Sayo spends a whole Tuesday unable to unsee a cat-print apron and unable to tell a single person about it, while the class escalates the vinegar theory to a family technique — and then she drops a delivery slip in the corridor, and the Ninth reads her name off the paper and says four words, and the largest social event of the term is over in under three seconds.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Ch1 ended unresolved at 05:12 with Sayo braked in the arcade and Kaoru in the apron with his arms in the bran. No agreement, no threat, no bargain. Both of them are carrying it alone.
- Honorific register stage 0 going in: Sayo has never addressed him; Kaoru has only ever seen KIKUCHI in a window. This chapter moves it to stage 1, once, off paper.
- Genzō has taken an order for six blades from "the barrel boy" due Friday. Sayo agreed to the job without knowing whose it is. She still does not know at the end of this chapter.
- The vinegar gag stands at "reagent", canonised by the class in ch1. Ch2's rung is "A TECHNIQUE." Kanta's bread for ch1 was Bottom Shelf.
