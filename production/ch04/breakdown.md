# CHAPTER 4 — GREET DOWN, NEVER UP

LOGLINE: Monday, and Tsujimura Ibuki — out of nothing but kindness — stands up in front of forty people and champions Rank 1 for her Group Assessment, so the projected average drops one-point-one before anyone has done a minute of work and Sayo spends the next forty minutes being grateful at people who are being lovely at her; then at 11:40, in the corridor outside the second-year hall, the Ninth says one word to the bottom of the seating chart, the building comes apart over it, and Sayo goes home understanding for the first time that his silence was never his idea.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Ch3 ended with terms: "NOBODY HEARS ABOUT THIS." / "OBVIOUSLY NOT." Sayo has been inside the shop, stirred the bran bed and seen the private face at full length. Neither of them refers to it here, once, in any register.
- Honorific register stage 2 going in: Sayo says "Fujinami-san" (arrived at it mid-fold in ch3, uncommented). The class still says "Fujinami-shi". Kaoru has said her name aloud exactly once, in the ch2 corridor, off paper — this chapter does not spend a second use.
- Vinegar gag stands at "BRINE," canonised by the class in ch3. Ch4 rung is "TUESDAY'S." Kanta's breads so far: Bottom Shelf, Regret, Left In.
- Ch1 and ch2 both ran the same failed template: Ibuki tries to help Sayo up to floor 3, cannot, offers a substitute, forgets. This chapter is that template escalating into a public success, which is worse. The Shuffle cold-open composition is reused from ch1 exactly: floor-level desk legs, then the bin, then KOTO.

## CONTINUITY NOTES
- Ground gained, and it is entirely interior: Sayo has reframed Kaoru. His coldness is not a personality — it is a rule that was applied to him and that he has been obeying for six hundred days. The reframe assembles across p12 (his palm wiped dry on his jacket, seen only by the reader), p17 (the emptied corridor, "IT ISN'T HIM.
