# CHAPTER 3 — NUKADOKO

LOGLINE: Friday, and the six-blade order Sayo took without knowing whose it was turns out to be Sakaechō 3 — so she walks into Fujinami Tsukemono holding a slip that is already hers, finds the Ninth behind the counter in a cat-print apron with his arms in a ninety-year-old rice-bran bed, and because a man who has not spoken to anyone in fourteen months has forgotten how to ask people to leave, she is handed a paddle instead.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Genzō took an order for six blades from "the barrel boy," due Friday; Sayo agreed to the job in ch1 without knowing whose it was. Neither lead has said out loud that the Friday order is his. This chapter is that Friday.
- THE SLIP is live: Sayo's own carbon copy, refolded along its three creases by Kaoru in the ch2 corridor event, with FRI - 6 BLADES - SAKAECHO 3 at the bottom. She has never read the Sakaechō line. "I came to return a slip" is therefore available and false, exactly as planned.
- Honorific register stage 1 going in: Sayo says "Fujinami-shi"; Kaoru has said "KIKUCHI" aloud exactly once, off paper. This chapter moves her to stage 2 — mid-chore, by accident, never commented.
- The vinegar gag stands at "a technique," canonised by the class in ch2 and escalated to soaking in a barrel. Ch3's rung is "BRINE." Kanta's breads so far: Bottom Shelf, Regret.
