# CHAPTER 1 — SEAT FORTY-ONE

LOGLINE: Sayo drags her desk to the back corner for the fourteenth month running, draws the most beautiful useless line anyone has seen in twenty years, and at 05:12 the next morning finds the boy from Seat 1 on the pavement in a cat-print apron, arguing with a bucket.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Nobody knows either lead's private face. Sayo says "Fujinami-shi"; Kaoru has never said her name aloud.
- Kaoru has held Seat 1 for fourteen months and has started no conversations for ~600 days; Sayo has held Seat 41 since she arrived.
- Genzō's right hand has begun to shake and nobody has said so, including him.
- Sui took "youngest Rank 9 on record" from Kaoru eight months ago.
