WORLD RENDER RULES (apply on every page):
- UNIFORM (all students): dark high-collar jacket, straight trousers or skirt, white under-shirt. On the LEFT CUFF: horizontal white RANK BARS, one per rank, stacked up the forearm — Rank 9 has nine bars nearly to the elbow, Rank 1 has one. Rank is legible in line art from the sleeve alone; how a character wears their sleeve is characterisation.
- TALLY WINDOWS: a small rectangular UI window rendered near a person's SHOULDER (never overhead) — thin single-weight box, hard monospace ALL-CAPS lettering, family name first ("FUJINAMI, KAORU / RANK 9 · SEAT 1 / 41"), four lines max, no exclamation marks, never inside a speech bubble. Windows are a POV effect: draw them only when someone in the panel is looking; an empty-street panel has no windows at all.
- SIGNING (the magic): an open stroke is a SOLID BLACK BRUSH LINE hanging in the air — tapered start, wet blunt terminal, real ink weight. When strokes close a shape it SEALS: the black inverts to WHITE with a thin screentone halo. Discharge: the white shape shatters into RADIAL HATCH LINES — no glow, no gradients, hatch only. A failed cast sags, drips, and breaks into small round dots that fall. Overworked hands are drawn in flat 20% tone with no interior linework.

CHARACTER LOCKS:

FUJINAMI KAORU (lead, 17, RANK 9) — 178cm, tallest of the students except Machi; lean, flat-shouldered, long forearms, stands too straight like someone corrected him for years. Hair: solid black, no tone, blunt straight fringe cut slightly too short across the brow, and a single stubborn cowlick at the crown that never lies down — always draw the cowlick. Eyes: narrow, heavy-lidded, very small dark pupils with almost no highlight, lower lash line one flat stroke — reads as bored, is actually mild social panic. Faint permanent shadow under both eyes. Uniform: worn correctly to the millimetre, collar hooked, jacket buttoned to the throat, but BOTH SLEEVES ROLLED TO THE ELBOW exposing all nine white rank bars on the left cuff. Props: a shop-issue folding knife (plain wooden handle) in his breast pocket; a hand towel tucked at his hip, always, even in uniform. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: the cat-print apron (fat white cat, screentone-grey stripes, one strap always twisted) over the school shirt, collar hook undone, cowlick doubled — and his hands are drawn IN MOTION (wiping, stirring, wringing); in public his hands are drawn STILL at his sides. Public = still hands. Private = busy hands.

KIKUCHI SAYO (lead, 17, RANK 1) — 158cm, twenty centimetres under Kaoru; compact and low-centred, with visibly developed forearms and wrists the uniform hides badly — draw the forearms honestly. Hair: mid-grey screentone (60%), chin-length blunt bob, side-parted, two cheap plastic clips over the right ear — the grey tone must read instantly against Kaoru's solid black in any two-shot. Eyes: large, round, high-set with TWO full highlights and a soft lower lid — maximum harmlessness. In private: the SAME eyes with highlights reduced to ONE small hard dot and the lower lid flattened; nothing else changes. Uniform: jacket a size too big, LEFT SLEEVE ALWAYS PULLED DOWN OVER THE KNUCKLES to cover her single rank bar, hem frayed from gripping. Props: a canvas roll of knives wrapped in blue shop cloth strapped to her bicycle rack; bandaged fingers (never the same finger twice, always at least one); whetstone grit in her palm creases. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: hair goes UP — twisted knot held with a pencil or chopstick, faded blue shop bandana across the brow, sleeves shoved to the elbow with the single white bar bare, feet planted wide. Public = sleeve down, hair down, hands hidden. Private = sleeve up, hair up, hands out.

TSUJIMURA IBUKI (17, RANK 8, class rep) — 165cm, upright, gestures with both hands. Hair: near-white / heavy highlight, long, half-up, a ribbon that matches nothing; big silhouette, reads as light against both leads. Eyes: large, bright, THREE highlights, thick upper lash — expensive-looking. Uniform: flawless, pressed, skirt exactly regulation, eight spotless rank bars. Props: a clipboard with the class list; a lint roller in her bag. PRIVATE-FACE TELL (night scenes only): oversized grey sweatshirt, sleeves past her hands, hair completely flat, eating standing up, screen-glow lit from below.

MEGURO KANTA (17, RANK 3) — 172cm, broad, slouched, hands in pockets or holding a paper bag. Hair: white/blond-read, cropped short with an undercut growing out unevenly. Eyes: small, wide-set, permanently mid-laugh, thick brows. Uniform: jacket open over a T-shirt, three rank bars, no tie. Props: a paper bakery bag, always, with a handwritten tag; flour on one sleeve. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: a cheap notebook he shoves into his waistband when anyone approaches; his ears drawn solid black when embarrassed.

AMENOMIYA SUI (16, RANK 9, first-year) — 152cm, smallest in the cast, slight, always drawn from a low angle so she looms anyway. Hair: very pale grey tone (20%), short, sharp asymmetric cut, one side tucked behind the ear. Eyes: enormous, pupils drawn as tiny black dots in a lot of white — bright and slightly wrong; a beautiful child's face with a machine's eyes. Uniform: worn perfectly, first-year piping, nine rank bars on a very small forearm. Props: an indexed black notebook; a water bottle she never puts down. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: kneeling on a practice-hall floor, hair sweat-flat, one hand braced, notebook open — her strokes in that state come out forked and dripping.

FUCHIDA MACHI (18, UNLISTED/Rank 0) — 180cm, tallest character in the series, heavy-shouldered, rounded, moves slowly. Hair: solid black, very long, unstyled, hanging forward in curtains hiding one eye and most of the face — she reads as a dark shape first; negative space is the design. Eyes (when visible): wide, calm, soft-lidded, low highlights. Uniform: third-year, loose and untucked, LEFT CUFF BARE — no bars at all. Props: a fat rubber-banded sketchbook with furred corners; a box of crackers. She "speaks" by holding up sketchbook pages lettered in handwriting — not bubbles. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: hair pushed behind one ear with a pencil while drawing (use at most once per chapter).

KIKUCHI GENZŌ (74, NO DATA) — 160cm and stooped, thick through the hands and wrists, everything else thin. Hair: white, screentone-free, drawn as line only, cropped; three days of stubble. Eyes: nearly closed by default, drawn as two curved lines; when they open, the whites are drawn and it's a shock. Shop coat over a vest, sleeves cuffed with rubber bands, no rank bars anywhere. Props: a whetstone worn into a saddle-shaped hollow; reading glasses pushed up on his head. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: his shaking right hand tucked into the opposite armpit whenever anyone comes in — background detail, unremarked.

FUJINAMI KOTOHA (9, PENDING) — 130cm, small and round-limbed, always in motion or asleep. Hair: solid black like Kaoru's, long, two low uneven bunches she ties herself, with THE IDENTICAL CROWN COWLICK — the family resemblance is one line of ink. Eyes: huge, round, FOUR highlights, the brightest eyes in the book. Primary-school smock over shorts, indoor shoes worn outdoors. Props: a stepstool she drags everywhere in the shop; a much smaller matching cat apron. She has no private-face tell — she is the only person in Nagihama with one face.

ICHINOSE MANABU (34, RANK 7, homeroom teacher/proctor) — 176cm, thin, permanently slightly stooped at the neck. Hair: grey-black tone (40%), receding at the temples, flattened on one side. Eyes: tired, half-lidded, deep lower shadow, small round glasses with the LENSES TONED SOLID GREY in every public panel. Cheap suit, jacket off, sleeves rolled, faculty armband, seven bars. Props: a curling printed class list on a clipboard; an unlit cigarette behind the ear. PRIVATE-FACE TELL: glasses off, pinching the bridge of his nose, eyes drawn clear with visible whites — only when he is about to say something honest.
